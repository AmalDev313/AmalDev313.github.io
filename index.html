ThreatRadar

An Android app that detects Wi-Fi attacks as they happen.

This was my MSc dissertation. ThreatRadar watches the network your phone is connected to and warns you when something is wrong. It detects four types of attack, and it keeps monitoring in the background.

The part I'm proudest of is the DNS hijacking check. It works on a normal, unrooted phone, and that method was the original contribution of the dissertation.

To prove it worked I built an attack bench: Kali Linux in VirtualBox with an Alfa AWUS036ACM adapter, running live wireless attacks against the phone.

MiniSOC

In progress

A security operations centre that fits on one laptop.

I wanted to practise the defender's side properly, so I built a lab where I can attack a machine and then go and find that attack in the logs.

A Kali attacker and a Metasploitable target sit on a host-only network in VirtualBox. The target forwards its system logs, plus the Apache logs from DVWA, to Splunk on the Windows host.

Still to come: detection rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, attack simulation with Atomic Red Team, and a written triage note for each alert.
